One repo, one card. The primary type comes from what the project does, the
secondary type from its language. HP and level are computed from its stars, forks,
topics and age. The attacks are its rarest topics — the ones that actually set it
apart. Nothing is invented. The sprite is computed from the repo name, so it will
never change.
